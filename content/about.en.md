> Follow my brain dump as I debug complex architectures, test new tools, and share real software engineering learnings.

**zerodowntime.info** is not just a blog, it's an open logbook. It's where I document my experiences dealing with infrastructure, automation, development, and the daily challenges of keeping systems running in high availability (hence *zero downtime*).

Here you will find:
- **Crash Reports:** What broke and how I fixed it.
- **Deep Dives:** Technical deep dives into specific technologies.
- **Snippets:** Useful code, scripts, and configurations I don't want to forget.
- **Homelab / Self-hosting:** Adventures in configuring home servers and personal infrastructure.
- **Tool Reviews:** My unfiltered opinions on CLIs, frameworks, and Cloud services.
- **DevOps Culture:** Reflections on automation, architecture, and continuous improvement processes.
- **Off-Topic / Randomness:** Thoughts on career, productivity, or any non-technical subject that also takes up space in my brain.

Feel free to explore the logs and use any code you find useful in your own ecosystem. System online. Ready.
